Andersland, I., Faye, R., & Norhagen, S. L. (2025). Placing religion virtually: Facilitating critical democratic literacy with 360-degree video in teacher education. British Journal of Religious Education, 1–15.
Tidsskriftartikkel (Fagfellevurdert)
Denne studien undersøker hvordan 360-graders video av hellige rom i VR-briller kan fungere som en meningsfull tilnærming til religionsundervisning i lærerutdanningen og fremme kritisk demokratisk kompetanse gjennom sanselig tilstedeværelse og felles faglig refleksjon.
Norhagen, S. L. (2025). The PDC Ladder for Teacher Education: A Three-Tiered Framework for Digital Competence Integration. I EDEN Europe 2025 Annual Conference Proceedings (s. 218). EDEN Digital Learning Europe.
Konferansebidrag & Poster
This poster and paper introduce the PDC Ladder for Teacher Education, a visual and conceptual model that distinguishes digital competence at three interconnected levels: pupils, teachers, and teacher educators. Based on Starkey’s model for teachers’ Professional Digital Competence and extended through Uerz et al.’s emphasis on modeling and metacommunication, the framework supports a clearer understanding of the complex role teacher educators play in preparing future teachers for the digital age.
Norhagen, S. L., Krumsvik, R. J., & Røkenes, F. M. (2024). Developing professional digital competence in Norwegian teacher education: A scoping review. Frontiers in Education, 9, Artikkel 1363529. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2024.1363529
Tidsskriftartikkel (Fagfellevurdert)
The widespread use of educational technology in schools compels teachers to seamlessly integrate technology while developing students’ digital competence. This scoping review investigates current research on Professional Digital Competence (PDC) and its implementation within Norwegian teacher education (2017–2022). Thirty-four peer-reviewed papers were identified and coded along three dimensions: (1) theoretical perspectives, (2) research questions and methods, and (3) application and implementation.
Norhagen, S. L. (2024). FJORD Framework: Integrating Student Perspectives Through Holistic Course Design. I Learning in the Age of AI: Towards Imaginative Futures – EDEN Europe 2024 Proceedings (s. 175–191).
Konferansebidrag & Poster
The FJORD framework (Flexible, Journey-Oriented, Remote Design) emerges from Norway as a transformative approach to educational course design for online and blended higher education. Derived from international methodologies and merged with local educational needs, this contribution introduces the model, highlighting its adaptability, learner-centered approach, and digital integration.
