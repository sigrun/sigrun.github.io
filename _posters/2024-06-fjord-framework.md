The **FJORD Framework** (*Flexible, Journey-Oriented, Remote Design*) emerges from Norway as a practical, team-based approach to educational course design for online and blended higher education.

### Background & Purpose

Derived from international learning design methodologies (such as ABC Learning Design and Carpe Diem) and adapted to Nordic higher education governance and workload frameworks, FJORD guides academic teams through a structured process from administrative framing to weekly student learning journeys.

### Core Principles

* **Student-Centered Workload Budgeting:** Translating ECTS credits into realistic student work hours across reading, lectures, collaborative activities, and assessment.
* **Visual Storyboarding:** Mapping the learning journey across themes, learning activities, teacher presence, media resources, and formative/summative assessment checkpoints.
* **Collaborative Shared Language:** Shifting course development from solitary syllabus writing to a facilitated, collegial design process.
