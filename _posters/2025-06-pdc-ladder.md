This poster introduces the **PDC Ladder for Teacher Education**, a visual and conceptual model that distinguishes digital competence at three interconnected levels: **pupils**, **teachers**, and **teacher educators**.

### Background & Conceptual Foundation

Based on Starkey’s (2020) model for teachers’ Professional Digital Competence and extended through Uerz et al.’s (2018) emphasis on modeling and metacommunication, the framework supports a clearer understanding of the complex double-layered role teacher educators play in preparing future teachers for the digital age.

### Three Interconnected Levels

1. **Primary School Level (Pupils):** Digital skills, subject-specific digital use, and foundational technological understanding in school.
2. **Teacher Level (Student Teachers & In-Service Teachers):** Managing the digital learning environment, using technology for teaching, critical evaluation of tools, and teaching pupils who use technology.
3. **Teacher Educator Level:** Metacognition and explicit modeling — making pedagogical and technological reasoning visible ("thinking aloud" about *why*, *when*, and *how* digital or analogue approaches are chosen).

### Key Takeaway for Teacher Educators

> Make digital competence visible. Think aloud. Invite reflection. Show the *why* behind the *how*.
